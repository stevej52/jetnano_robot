XIAO RP2040 relay watchdog: wiring

Four wires. The XIAO sits between Jetson header pin 7 and the relay's IN. Relay closes only while pin 7 toggles at 20 Hz; it opens 0.2 s after the toggling stops.

The four wires

FromToWhy
Jetson header pin 7XIAO D0the 20 Hz heartbeat from the servo node (3.3 V both sides)
XIAO D1relay IN (move it off pin 7)the XIAO switches the relay from now on
XIAO 5VPCA9685 V+ (5.19 V, the relay's supply)powers the XIAO
XIAO GNDPCA9685 GNDcommon ground with the Jetson and the relay
10 kΩ (optional)D0 to GNDholds the input low while the Jetson boots and pin 7 floats
Do not plug the XIAO's USB into a computer while its 5V pin is wired to V+. Its 5V pin is tied straight to USB power; the two supplies would fight. Pull the 5V wire first, every time you reprogram it.

Order of work

  1. Program first, wire later. Hold the XIAO's B button while plugging it into the laptop over USB-C. A drive RPI-RP2 appears.
  2. Drag the CircuitPython .uf2 for the XIAO RP2040 onto it (circuitpython.org/board/seeeduino_xiao_rp2040). It comes back as CIRCUITPY.
  3. Copy firmware/relay_watchdog/code.py from the repo onto CIRCUITPY, replacing the one there. Unplug it.
  4. Robot off. Move the relay's IN wire from pin 7 to XIAO D1. New wire pin 7 → D0. Then 5V and GND to the servo board.
  5. Tell me it is in. I turn on output_enable_heartbeat_hz: 20.0 in pca9685.yaml and we run the four tests, wheels up: start-up click, stop from ROS, frozen node (relay drops in 0.2 s), XIAO unplugged (everything goes limp).
